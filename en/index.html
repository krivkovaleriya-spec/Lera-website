09 — questions

Frequently asked questions

If your answer isn't here — message me on WhatsApp or Telegram and I'll reply personally.

How much does an AI seller on WhatsApp or Telegram cost?+
From $630. That includes training the AI agent on your product, CRM integration (AmoCRM, Bitrix24), connecting to WhatsApp via Wazzup or to Telegram, testing and handover. Final price depends on scenario complexity and the number of integrations.
How is an AI seller different from a regular chatbot?+
A regular chatbot follows rigid button-driven scripts — the client walks through a predefined funnel. An AI seller powered by GPT or Claude has a real conversation: it understands context, handles objections, qualifies the client and drives them to payment. It also processes voice messages, files and images.
Who sets up GetCourse for online schools?+
I set up GetCourse for online schools across Kazakhstan, Russia and the CIS. 10+ years of experience, 50+ projects. Custom dashboard design, processes, tariffs, integrations with Tilda and payment systems. Contact: WhatsApp or Telegram.
Can the AI agent be connected to AmoCRM or Bitrix24?+
Yes. The AI agent works with AmoCRM out of the box — creating deals, moving them through the pipeline, adding tags and custom fields. Bitrix24 works via webhooks or built-in integrations. It also connects to Google Sheets, Notion and Telegram notifications for your managers.
What platforms is the AI seller built on?+
Two options. Code version — Node.js + Claude API (Anthropic), deployed on the client's VPS: maximum flexibility, voice via Whisper, multimodality. No-code on n8n — faster to build, easier to iterate on, fits most use cases.
How long does it take to launch an AI seller?+
Between 5 and 14 days. Stages: brief and product research (1–2 days), building the AI agent and populating the knowledge base (3–7 days), testing and adjustments (2–3 days), deployment and handover (1 day).
Do you build Tilda websites for online courses?+
Yes. Landing pages for launches, multi-page sites, membership clubs. Full cycle: design in Tilda, copy structure, responsive layout, integration with GetCourse and payment systems. From $320. Examples are in the "Cases" section above.
What is n8n and why does an online school need it?+
n8n is a no-code automation platform (similar to Zapier, but self-hosted). For an online school it automates lead delivery from Tilda into GetCourse and CRM, WhatsApp/Telegram broadcasts, payment processing, audience segmentation, and reporting into Google Sheets.
How much does a full GetCourse setup for an online school cost?+
From $320. Includes: domain setup, course and training structure, tariffs and access rules, integration with payment systems, custom student dashboard visuals. Delivered with a walkthrough — you know exactly how to manage the platform yourself.
How fast can you deliver a Tilda website?+
A landing page — 3 to 7 days after receiving all materials. A multi-page site — 7 to 14 days. Timing depends on scope and approval speed. Speed is one of the main things clients call out in their reviews.
Do you work with international clients?+
Yes, I work fully remotely with clients anywhere in the world. Current clients are in Kazakhstan, Russia, the United Kingdom and Cyprus. Payment in USD, EUR, GBP, RUB or KZT — whichever works for you.
